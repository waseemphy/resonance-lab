1

Below resonance
The oscillator follows the driving force with a relatively small phase lag.

2

Near resonance
Energy transfer is most effective and the response amplitude is greatest.

3

Above resonance
The amplitude falls and displacement becomes nearly opposite in phase to the force.

Effect of damping: greater damping lowers and broadens the resonance peak.
